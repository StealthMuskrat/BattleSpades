# Objective achievements and asynchronous result capture

This batch composes several source rule families with the existing standard host owners:

- Zombie sub-round survivor/MVP awards, three human kills in a round, last-survivor kill thresholds and survival time. MVP comparisons include every connected player's eligible cross-team kill tally, including bots; the configured bot-victim policy independently gates achievement credit.
- VIP MVP and pacifist awards. Each sub-round clears its own counters, ties can qualify, and match timeout resolves any still-open VIP round against current VIP identity and survival state.
- Diamond Mine fresh pickup, finder theft, dropped-object interception and three personally found diamonds cashed in within a match. Carry history follows objective serials through pickups and drops.
- Occupation spawn-to-base delivery, surviving an inside detonation, retaining a successful interception, and the winning team's final planter. Attacker recovery invalidates an interception.
- CTF carrier interception followed by an actual return. A subsequent pickup, repeated return, transition death, disconnect or team mismatch cannot reuse the credit.
- Multi-Hill sole scorer, contest retention/interception, shooting in/out, airstrike survival and staying inside throughout the strike. Captured player objects and zone bounds retain identity; the source one-second minimum and fifteen-second maximum remain authoritative.

The existing external observations still run after guarded achievement hooks. Match and sub-round counters are distinct from durable lifetime counters and fractional measurements. Match reset clears transient objective histories and candidates; a disconnected player cannot transfer an award to a replacement that reuses the same numeric slot.

`HostedRoundResults` automatically uses the bound `HostedAchievements` service. Score and participation deltas are reserved synchronously into immutable result evidence before any storage wait. Achievement eligibility is captured at that same boundary, including pending one-shot requests and reached lifetime tiers. A delayed earlier result cannot consume an unlock first earned in the next round. The worker performs a selective, durable one-time handoff, then the owner enriches the reserved envelope and offers it to the existing durable publisher. Captures are ordered and bounded together with publisher reservations. A failed handoff retains the capture and any already collected account handoffs for retry. Shutdown drains captures before closing the publisher and achievement worker. The legacy synchronous callback remains available to embeddings without a hosted achievement service; offline reporting still resolves no account claims.

The achievement store and result outbox are separate durable stores, as in the original handoff design. The host retains completed handoffs in memory until the outbox accepts their envelopes and awaits persistence at shutdown. Abrupt process loss between those two durable transactions is not made atomic by this batch.

Differential evidence extends the existing achievement generator with fourteen intact original-engine objective/sub-round traces. They cover positive awards, bot competition, sub-round and match resets, objective recovery and retaking, one-time intel return, transition death exclusion, sole/shared hill scorers, leaving a strike zone and dying during a strike. An actual UDP integration pauses the real achievement worker across two result captures and a round reset, admits another client during that wait, verifies that the second round's unlock stays with its own result, and awaits outbox and achievement shutdown.

The follow-up [environmental achievement checkpoint](ACHIEVEMENT_ENVIRONMENT.md) implements explosion scopes, authored-region evaluation, terrain/crate achievements and demolition damage/final damage. Ordinary standalone hosting and supported-client acceptance remain incomplete. The catalog's 77 definitions are not a claim that every rule is implemented. macOS testing remains deferred until full migration completion.
