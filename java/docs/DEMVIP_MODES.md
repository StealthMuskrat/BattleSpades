## Demolition

The standard factory owns `dem` with authored or safe-ground fallback base zones. Build time comes from mode settings or RULE_BUILD_STATE_LENGTH; authoritative PlayerPhysics bounds and native LockToZone(108) are applied on start, spawn, team change and late reveal, then released on activation/deactivation. Newly built cells are frozen only at build completion. Authored volumes include their real solid cells; fallback bases freeze the compact surface core. Base health tracks actual committed world topology, including collapsed cells and repairs, through independent subscribers that coexist with the canonical deployable removal hook. Paint does not change health. MinimapZone(43) carries native lock state; TeamProgress(117) carries removed/total objective counts while ordinary team score bars are hidden. Repair warnings are edge-triggered.

Enemy destruction and friendly repair credit use frozen membership, actual slot identity, per-50-cell score intervals and profile totals. Self-dig/refill farming is excluded. Required repair observations reach the external achievement owner; synchronous enemyObjectiveDamage exposes actual frozen enemy-cell counts to existing block observers. Defend/assault kills use the original objective classifier. Fractional remaining health decides timeout. Simultaneously destroyed bases draw, with no iteration-order winner. The recovered five-second warning precedes five immediate ownerless native shells per destroyed base; spatial siren/flyby cues, real terrain/HP blast owners and team-player POIFocus(18) are connected. Results wait three seconds after launch for impacts.

## VIP

The standard factory owns `vip` with ordinary gangster admission, locked class packets and live boss promotion. Promotion retains the actual Character, position, direction, generation, death history and current block wallet; it refreshes equipment, native class/loadout, capped HP/max HP and through-wall crown markers. The configured boss health and half-damage modifier apply to real lives. Native boss deaths use kill type 26 and the no-respawn sentinel 255. Ready candidates must be settled clients or bots, and object identities prevent recycled compact ids inheriting crowns or credits. Chicago uses the updated source's surveyed full spawn volumes with live floor validation, cached candidates, re-enumeration and the standard safe fallback.

Each boss death immediately awards one opposing team point and team-relative cues/radar. Bereaved teams cannot respawn or switch out; late arrivals enter dead and receive VIP_ALREADY_DEAD. After six seconds, the sub-round is capped to one minute and real owner HP loses one point per second, including the native damage-type-4 flash, god-mode exemption, healing cooldown stamp and unattributed fatal transition. The target score leaves time for an answering kill; a target-level tie ends the match immediately as a draw. Timed/eliminated sub-rounds do not add extra points or open an early map ballot. Radar persists through intermission and is cleared at the next round. Cancellable owner timers start bounded respawn queues; live reset bodies are replaced without invented combat deaths. VIP disconnects hand the crown to a current teammate or resolve as death according to policy, with roster audits as a safety net. Periodic survival/escort, defend/distraction/assault, recent attackers and boss-kill bonuses use live scoreboard/profile owners. Required start/finish observations reach the external achievement owner; bot retirement safety is exposed for the remaining bot director.
